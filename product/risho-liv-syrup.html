Understanding Liver Health

The liver is an important organ that helps process nutrients, supports metabolism and performs several functions necessary for the body. Maintaining general health includes balanced nutrition, healthy habits and appropriate medical attention when needed. Liver-related symptoms can have different causes and should not be self-diagnosed.
