Understanding Liver Health

The liver performs several important functions in the body, including processing nutrients, producing certain proteins and helping the body process substances. Liver-related symptoms may have different causes and should not be self-diagnosed. Persistent digestive problems, unusual tiredness or concerns about liver health should be evaluated by a qualified healthcare professional.
